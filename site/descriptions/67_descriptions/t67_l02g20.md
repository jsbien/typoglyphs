# Unicode status
<pre> 
The glyph can be considered as a variant of the Latin brevigraph
for `el`, `ul` and `vel` and encoded as
ꝉ
LATIN SMALL LETTER L WITH HIGH STROKE (U+A749)
</pre>
# Description
<pre> 
In "A. Glaber, Gadki z pisma wielkiego filozofa Arystotela,
Kraków 1535." and perhaps also elsewhere used as Polish `ł`.
</pre>
